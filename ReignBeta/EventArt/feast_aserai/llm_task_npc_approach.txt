Write the moment this NPC approaches the player and opens interaction.
If more than one NPC is already active, acknowledge the social interruption instead of ignoring it.
Let personality guide confidence: bold characters can interrupt or invite; cautious characters should approach indirectly or with a polite pretext.
Use recent ConversationHistory when it matters. Be specific and personal; avoid generic greetings unless the NPC truly has no prior context.
Give the NPC a visible reason to approach now: music, seating, a stall, a toast, a social slight, curiosity, rivalry, courtesy, or a remembered thread.
Do not recycle topics from recent conversations unless the current event gives a fresh reason to raise them.
Do not force acceptance of dances, bargains, favors, secrets, or insults. Leave the player room to respond.
