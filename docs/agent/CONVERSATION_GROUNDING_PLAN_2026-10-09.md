## Causes and affected paths

1. **Observable input is silently discarded.** `ReignServer/src/Modules/Dialogue/PlayerInputAudience.cs` projects the current contribution and overwrites provider-bound input with `ObserverConversationText`. `ConversationObserverProjection.cs` accepts narrated sentences only when a narrow action expression matches. `reaches?` does not match bare `reach`; the inspected expressions also omit `pluck`, `use`, `mock duel` and `massaging`. Both reported sentences lose their entire action span. This matches the retained wire inputs. The same projection is reused for history and observable memory publication, so restoring only the latest prompt would leave follow-up recall incomplete.
2. **Narrative setting never becomes the shared authority.** `ConversationSceneState.cs` builds the shared venue from resolved player state or native location. Preparation currently creates no new scene proposals and relies on model-returned `sceneStateUpdates`. The stables opening returns an empty update, leaving a contradictory Tavern authority for the next turn. Existing `ConversationContinuity.cs` handoffs can preserve a recorded override; they cannot preserve a setting that was never recorded. Shared venue selection also needs to reconcile player and speaker updates together.
3. **Town presence has no party-membership dimension.** `ReignBeta/src/Modules/Platform/Integration/ReignServerClient.cs:3573` builds local presence from participants, relatives, mission agents and settlement heroes without parties. Its rows distinguish settlement, room and conversation witnesses, but omit party membership. `ConversationContinuity.cs:601` renders those same dimensions. Current contradiction checks principally catch explicit claims about known absent people; they do not establish or enforce the travelling roster.

## Implementation sequence

### 1. Preserve observable player actions through every stage

- Store the immutable original contribution before projection, linked to its turn/exchange, author, timeline, ordering and content hash. Preserve every character, action span and its position among spoken text. Distinguish submitted/pending from accepted turns so failure, cancellation or retry cannot fabricate an accepted event or duplicate it. Request-local storage alone is insufficient for durable history.
- Make the player's displayed conversation, reopened history and exports render that original. Keep the original and each NPC's observer projection in separate fields/records; never overwrite the canonical source with the filtered string. A private thought may remain in the player's own record without being delivered to an NPC.
- Retain ordered source spans for speech, visible action, private thought, quoted/historical action and hypothetical intent. Keep audience/whisper filtering independent of action recognition. Every source span must remain accounted for by an original-source reference and an explicit projection disposition. Missing/unclassified is a diagnostic failure, not permission to silently discard the span.
- Repair verb inflections and replace whole-sentence rejection with clause-aware projection. An unfamiliar physical verb must not silently erase an otherwise observable interaction. Preserve original wording and qualifiers; do not turn imagined or remembered acts into present facts by deleting their qualifiers.
- Handle mixed clauses: visible contact may remain while an unspoken interpretation is excluded. `Mindlessly` can qualify a visible gesture without making the gesture invisible. Pretend swordplay with grass is an observable pantomime, without being a real duel or an imaginary-only thought.
- Carry relevant action spans into the actual serialized writer input, accepted observable turns, scene handoffs, summaries and later recall. Keep author-only mental material private and preserve source hashes/offsets.
- Require a natural response to a material action directed at the NPC. Temurtai can accept, tense, object or move away from the massage; Corein can join, tease or decline the grass duel. Do not force consent, enjoyment, healing, a relationship gain or a native combat action. Incidental gestures need not all receive repetitive acknowledgments.
- Audit prior affected conversations read-only for differences between original inputs, accepted/displayed turns and observer views. Recover the player's exact history from retained source-only originals or exact diagnostic records with matching identities/hashes when available. Report unrecoverable gaps; never reconstruct wording from a summary or invent an NPC reaction. Review the exact repair set before any historical write, and project recovered actions only to historically eligible observers.

Primary implementation: `ConversationObserverProjection.cs`, its self-tests, `PlayerInputAudience.cs`, and the relevant prompt/persistence integration contracts. Keep this work separate from the active Corein long-term memory retrieval repair.

- Distinguish a real native location/room transition from the unchanged background room in repeated payloads. Respect existing logical-session lifecycle and clothing rules; the fix must not teleport the native party, mutate equipment or leak an old scene into another conversation/branch.
