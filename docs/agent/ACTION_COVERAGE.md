## Contextual interfaces and limits

Court docket judgments, petition grants/refusals, proclamations, court-life/family/visitor/patronage decisions, training-yard sessions and event scheduling also exist as contextual interfaces. Their owning code includes `ReignNobleDocketCampaignBehavior`, `ReignRulerDocketCampaignBehavior`, `ReignCourtLifeCampaignBehavior`, `ReignTrainingYardScreenVM` and event campaign behaviors. These are not silently counted as new sentence-builder commands. Their dedicated decision buttons and feature harnesses remain available; general sentence/console adapters for those contexts are follow-up coverage. Generic asset transfer or a Native primitive is not proof of the complete contextual workflow.

Internal lifecycle, initialization, save/load, cleanup, server synchronization, portrait generation, memory maintenance and test-fixture setup are not interpersonal bargain actions. Existing original Reign tools own those operations and their gates. Drinking, intimacy/pregnancy and non-native social gifts retain the agreed social exception. No GameMaster implementation or dependency is incorporated.

## Evidence requirements

Canonical compilation, shared contracts, metadata parity, preview/fidelity audits and native acceptance are separate proof layers. The 358-command ledger contains four native passes, one retained failure awaiting retest, and 353 pending entries as of the rebellion addition. Testing uses the user's BaseThree baseline through the verified task-owned Current; the baseline stays protected. For each row, retain resolved inputs, source/artifact identity, campaign/timeline/save identity, preflight, receipt and independent assertion of its intended effect. Destructive or lifecycle primitives need their applicable isolated setup; do not invoke them against an unsuitable state merely to tick a row.

The guarded operator harness collects evidence without providers. It does not establish NPC willingness, human visual acceptance or a fresh-process persistence round trip. A deferred objective must be observed at fulfillment; ambiguous results remain pending or failed. Required recovery, consent, stale-revision, many-to-many conservation and replay cases supplement the per-command happy path.
