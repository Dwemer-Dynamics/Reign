## Identity and content

`narrative.json` is the complete authored source for life, voice and rated personal concerns. Native biography, family, traits, MBTI and native skills retain their existing authority. The v5 shipped catalog references a separate narrative file per hero; it becomes usable only when every referenced narrative is complete. The v4 catalog is preserved for older campaigns.

The authored library contains 664 hobbies in 32 families and 2,096 other concepts. Childhood concepts describe play, care and emerging expectations. Adult concepts include practical, intellectual, social, artistic and animal interests alongside varied moral and personal motives. Availability reflects native age; familiar native skills modestly affect hobby opportunities without conferring expertise. Verified native display names and omitted ages are recorded with source hashes in `ProfileLibrary/narrative/native-context.json`.

Per-character starting ranges are 3–7 hobbies, 4–8 formative experiences, 2–5 dreams, 2–5 desires, 2–6 fears, 2–5 values and 1–4 attachments. Wounds, jealousy, temptation and private truths each range from zero to three. The provider must make the selected concepts coherent with the individual; it cannot omit concerns, change their ratings, fabricate named native events or substitute generic completion.

Every concern has a title, individual description, personal meaning, semantic identity, disclosure boundary and whole-number importance from 1 to 10. Starting probabilities for levels 1 through 10 are 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 20%, 12%, 8%, 5%, 3% and 2%. Stable SHA-256 draws choose initial content and break ties. The three strongest distinct meanings accompany the native top-skill prompt. A fear and a desire expressing the same underlying aim occupy one slot. No category quota applies. Speech style, social mask and tells are presentation traits and cannot enter the defining trio. Additional relevant concerns can enter the live prompt.

Importance shapes relevant choices and reactions; it does not dictate recurring dialogue topics. Immediate danger, responsibilities, native facts, privacy and sound judgment remain binding. Interest does not grant skill, fear does not prove danger and jealousy does not prove wrongdoing. A ruler's consuming fondness for kittens must not interrupt an unrelated military supply council. Rated values are priorities rather than automatically absolute moral prohibitions.

When duties are finished and a strong interest is relevant, it should receive sustained attention and concrete curiosity in that character's voice. Current ratings govern current strength even when historical examples describe occasional practice. The v5 foundation uses the defining trio and retrieves additional relevant concerns; it does not repeat the entire legacy motivation list as equally prominent priorities.

## Construction and continuity

The native campaign behavior persists the narrative version. Only a newly created campaign starts at v5; a missing older save marker remains legacy. Server campaign metadata supplies this version to unencountered profiles, while an explicit native profile marker wins.

Shipped v5 profiles materialize without provider calls. The initial roster is authored within the implementation task, not through the game's construction provider. Valid sections and concerns survive checkpointed targeted repairs (at most three total requests); provider failures stop immediately. Completed compatible profiles from the earlier protocol are reused. Publication still requires a complete validated document.

Accepted development is an override over the immutable starting item. The NPC must actually express a lasting response in its visible reply, and native compatibility must pass. Acceptance additionally requires a personally relevant major event from the active completed native history, or coherent evidence from three separate encounters spanning thirty campaign days. Player assertions and repeated turns in one encounter are insufficient. Save Sync includes narrative identity and development as temporal state.

The editor presents the effective current values and retains history. Saving an unchanged effective item preserves the original baseline and its development. An explicit correction supersedes overrides based on the old item without deleting their history. Development invalidates open editor revisions and prompt caches. Active narrative overrides are retained even when ordinary characteristic display rows exceed their projection limit.
