## Compact sentence interface

- Alt+A opens the same compact editor from every Reign conversation. The initial choices are **I would like you** and **I wish to**.
- Reveal one choice at a time: verb, compatible asset/target category, searchable alphabetical scrolling dropdown, then quantity or other required terms. Bound the list height; never expand a world-sized list across the screen.
- Keep the sentence visible and let the player revisit its parts. Back preserves earlier choices; Cancel discards this draft and returns focus to the unchanged conversation.
- A sentence can end after its first complete clause: **I wish to give you 100 denars.** Accept adds it to speech; it does not manufacture NPC consent.
- Insert the accepted sentence at the current chat caret as one indivisible white action segment. Keep ordinary speech editable before and after it. Backspace, Delete, cut, or replacement touching the segment removes the entire segment and cancels its attached draft. Text that merely resembles an action is not executable. Reopening and cancelling the builder preserves the original segment. Insertion alone performs no gameplay mutation.
- An optional continuation **and you will give…** (or **and I will give…**) splits the sentence into a bargain. Do not require a two-sided trade screen for a simple gift/request. Each side supports repeated **and…** additions, mixed asset classes, multiple troop types, exact item variants and quantities.
- Show only the current selector and a compact summary. Longer agreements scroll within a bounded review area. Relevant unavailable options follow available choices at the bottom, disabled with a reason.
- Use the approved Reign black marble, antique gold and dynamic serif typography. Reuse complete owning assets and bound every scroll region. Native confirmation and approved-reference evidence remain separate from browser proof.
